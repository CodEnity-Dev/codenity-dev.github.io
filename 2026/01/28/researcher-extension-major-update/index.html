The Researcher Extension: New Features & Updates

We’re excited to announce a major update to The Researcher extension! This release brings powerful new features designed to supercharge your research workflow across Chrome, Firefox, and Edge browsers.

🎉 What’s New in v2.1.0

Enhanced Search Intelligence

  • AI-Powered Suggestions: Get smarter search recommendations based on your research context
  • Cross-Reference Detection: Automatically identify related topics and sources
  • Search History Analytics: Understand your research patterns with detailed insights

Improved Note-Taking

  • Rich Text Editor: Format your research notes with full styling support
  • Auto-Save: Never lose your research with intelligent auto-saving
  • Tag System: Organize findings with customizable tags and categories

Performance Optimizations

  • 50% Faster Loading: Streamlined codebase for lightning-quick responses
  • Reduced Memory Usage: More efficient resource management
  • Better Mobile Support: Enhanced experience on mobile browsers

BillKoto Your Bangladesh electricity bill, simplified

BillKoto helps households in Bangladesh estimate monthly electricity costs using tariff slabs, appliance usage, and sanctioned load. It provides a fast calculator, clear breakdowns, and history tracking so users can understand and control energy costs.

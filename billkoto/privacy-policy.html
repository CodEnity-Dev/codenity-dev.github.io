Privacy Policy for BillKoto

Last updated: 2026-05-29

This Privacy Policy explains how BillKoto ("App") collects, uses, and shares information. The App is provided by CodEnity ("we", "us", "our").

If you have questions, contact: codenity.developer@gmail.com

Summary
  • We use Firebase services for analytics, crash reporting, performance monitoring, and remote configuration.
  • We use Google AdMob to display ads.
  • We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data such as national ID, bank details, or health data.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

Information We Collect

1) App Usage and Diagnostics

We collect usage and diagnostic data to improve stability and performance. This may include:

  • App interaction events (screen views, button taps)
  • App performance metrics (load times, startup performance)
  • Crash logs and error reports
  • Device information (OS version, device model, language, approximate location from IP)

Services used: Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Performance Monitoring.

2) Advertising Data

We use Google AdMob to serve ads. AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID)
  • Device and app usage data for ad delivery and measurement

For more information, see Google's policies:

  • https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

3) Local Storage

We store user preferences and app data locally on your device (for example, settings, saved calculations, and appliance lists). This data does not leave your device unless you choose to share it.

How We Use Information

  • Provide and maintain app features
  • Diagnose and fix crashes
  • Understand usage trends to improve the app
  • Measure and improve app performance
  • Serve and measure ads

Sharing of Information

We share data only with service providers required to operate the app:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance, Remote Config)
  • Google AdMob (Ads)

We do not sell personal data to third parties.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us to request deletion.

Your Choices

  • You can limit ad personalization in your device settings.
  • You can reset your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • You can stop all data collection by uninstalling the app.

Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide and improve the service. Some analytics or crash data may be retained by Firebase per their policies.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in this file with an updated date.
